# Cue sheet contract

Each beat needs the shot ID, clean spoken line, display tokens, prop already visible, exact anchor token, first meaningful motion, contact/reveal, camera focus and exit state. Record absolute start, speech end and cut time in seconds after synthesis.

| Spoken moment | Before | On the word | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| “एक कीड़ा” | The dirt moves. | A worm breaks the surface on “keeda.” | Let it retract after the viewer registers it. |
| “टपका” | The acorn falls. | It touches ground and a quiet tap sounds. | Hold it in the frame for the next decision. |
| “तीन सौ” | The ticket has entered the shot. | Its price is large enough to read. | Keep the price fixed while the next hour ticks away. |

Check continuity with a state ledger. Track who has each prop, the direction an actor faces, where the sun is and what the camera last showed. An acorn carried off by the bird must disappear from the pile. Captions need room in the predictable fixed band even when the camera changes scale. Let a pause go caption-free once speech ends.
